# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-25)

First preview: the NoteHawk shell in Bytehawk's Hawkline style.

- Hawkline sidebar: New note, search, Home, Notes, Notebooks, Tasks, Calendar, Tags, Shared, Templates, Trash, a notebook list, light/dark mode and Settings.
- Home (Evernote-style): greeting with quick actions, recent notes, scratch pad, tasks, pinned notes, notebooks and tags.
- Notes (OneNote + Evernote): section tabs across the top, a list of pages, and the page itself with a full formatting toolbar.
- Three sample notebooks to browse. Editing, saving, search and sync are marked coming soon.
